## Hearth 0.3.0: Agents

See what every coding agent on your Mac is doing, in one line, written on your Mac.

### New: Agents
- **Claude Code and Codex sessions in the menu bar panel**, with their project, state (Working, Permission,
  Your turn, Waiting, Quiet) and how long they've been in it. Subagents such as a reviewer show under their
  session, and background work like a dev server gets a "running: …" note.
- **A one-line summary per session**, written by Apple Intelligence on this Mac from the session's own events.
  No transcripts are read and nothing leaves the Mac. Permission requests, waiting on a subagent and stopped
  turns are written exactly, without the model.
- **A dot on the menu bar flame when an agent needs you**: at once for a permission request, and after 10 seconds
  of a finished turn waiting for your reply.
- **Cards in the Details window**: the same sessions with room to read, and each session's last 20 events.
- **Connect / Disconnect** from the panel or the gear menu. Hearth adds its hooks to `~/.claude/settings.json` or
  `~/.codex/hooks.json`, keeps a backup next to the file every time, and removes exactly its own hooks on
  Disconnect. Codex asks once to review the hooks.
- **Never in the way.** The hooks exit at once with no output whether Hearth is running, quit or stuck, and
  nothing about your sessions is written to disk or logs.

Tested with Claude Code 2.1.319 and Codex 0.167 (CLI and app).

### Fixed
- The panel now closes when you click outside it, also after using a command from its gear menu (since 0.1.0).
- The menu bar item no longer disappears while the server is paused; it shows a dimmed flame (since 0.1.0).

### Known limits
- After Esc in Claude Code, the session's row updates with your next prompt.
- When Hearth starts, sessions that are already open appear with their next event.

### Install
1. Download `Hearth-0.3.0.dmg` below, open it and drag Hearth to Applications (replacing 0.2.0 is fine).
2. This build isn't notarized yet. The first time you open it, macOS says it can't verify the app: close that
   message, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 15 or later; Apple Intelligence (and the agent summaries) need macOS 26.
